How to use this panel

Quick answers for common changes.

How do I add a new opportunity guide?

Go to New Guide, fill the fields, and click Publish. A full SEO page is created at /opportunities/your-slug/ and added to your sitemap. After it's live, go to Google Search Console and "Request Indexing" for the new URL so it ranks sooner.

How do I change my pricing or any text?

Go to Pages & Files → open index.html → use your browser's Find (Ctrl+F) inside the box to locate the text → edit it → Save. Live in ~1 minute.

I think I broke something — how do I undo?

Every change is saved in GitHub. Open your repo → click the file → History → open the previous version → copy its content → paste back here and Save. Nothing is ever lost.

How do I link a new guide on the hub page?

Open opportunities/index.html in Pages & Files, find the right category section, and copy an existing guide-card block, then change its link, title and text to point to your new guide.

Changes aren't showing?

Wait ~1 minute (GitHub Pages rebuilds), then hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) or open in a private window. Cloudflare cache can take a few minutes.
