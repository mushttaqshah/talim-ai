About Syed Mushtaq Ur Rehman Shah Bukhari

Syed Mushtaq Ur Rehman Shah Bukhari — known professionally as Mushtaq Shah — is the Founder and CEO of Talim AI, an education-technology platform that brings AI to accreditation, compliance, and student opportunity across Pakistani higher education. He built Talim AI because, after years inside university administration, he saw that the tooling accreditation actually requires simply did not exist.

Mushtaq serves as Principal's Coordinator at Bahria University's Bahria Business School (BBS-IC) in Islamabad — effectively the strategic nerve centre of the Principal's office. There he quality-assures institutional reports, runs a real-time performance dashboard across every academic program, and acts as the primary liaison between the Principal's office and the university's HR, Academics, Finance, and Quality Assurance directorates.

Accreditation & quality assurance

His core expertise is accreditation. Mushtaq has led institutional documentation and compliance coordination for HEC, NBEAC, and AACSB across departments — hands-on evidence collection, standards monitoring, and Peer Review Team (PRT) visit preparation across all seven NBEAC standards. He has collaborated directly with AACSB teams on Assurance of Learning (AoL) processes and accreditation-critical submissions, and has audited the agendas and minutes of statutory bodies (DBOS, DRC, ECM, ACM, HERC, and the Faculty Board of Studies) for full institutional and HEC compliance.

Entrepreneurship

Alongside his university role, Mushtaq co-founded Ebet Consulting within the Bahria Innovation Center's incubation ecosystem, leading its business development and digital presence. That blend of enterprise-grade administrative discipline and entrepreneurial agility is what shapes Talim AI today: practical tools, built by someone who has done the work by hand.

Education

Mushtaq holds an MS in Agribusiness Management from the National University of Sciences & Technology (NUST), Islamabad, and a BS in Food Science & Technology from Cholistan University of Veterinary & Animal Sciences. He has also gained corporate exposure through the PepsiCo Roshan Kal internship and served as a Public Relations intern under the Prime Minister's Youth Internship Programme.

About Talim AI

Through Talim AI, Mushtaq is building two things at once: AI tools that help Pakistani universities manage accreditation and compliance (NBEAC, AACSB, HEC), and free, practical resources that help students find scholarships, analyse their CVs, prepare for exams, and discover government youth opportunities. You can explore the platform on the Talim AI home page or browse the free opportunities hub.
