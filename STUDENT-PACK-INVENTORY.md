## 🏷️ Activation Strategy

**Rule of thumb:** Activate within 1 week of when you'll actively use the resource. Activations with time clocks (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year) start counting immediately — wasting months of access is the most common mistake.

**Right now (today/this week):** Cloudflare, SimpleAnalytics, Sentry, Imgbot, JetBrains, 1Password

**When you start CV Analyzer build:** Appwrite, Clerk, Doppler

**When you build the paid product:** DigitalOcean (or Azure), MongoDB

**Before going public with paid product:** AstraSecurity, BrowserStack, LambdaTest

**For marketing assets:** IconScout, Visme

**Never waste these — they're the most valuable:**
- DigitalOcean $200 credit (don't activate until you have a real backend to run)
- Microsoft Azure $100 (same)
- BrowserStack 1 year (start clock only when you have something to test)
- New Relic / Datadog (start only when production is live)
