NAVTTC free courses are government-funded skills training programmes run by the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission under the Prime Minister's Youth Skill Development Program. The courses are completely free, often pay a small monthly stipend, and give you a toolkit and a nationally and internationally recognised certificate. You apply online through the official NSIS portal.

What is the NAVTTC free training program?

NAVTTC is a federal body under the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training. Its free-training scheme equips young people with employable, market-driven skills — from IT and freelancing to technical trades — so they can find jobs in Pakistan or abroad, or start their own work. New batches open through the year and are announced on the official portal.

Benefits

  • Training is 100% free — no admission or course fee.
  • A monthly stipend for trainees (amount is set per batch; attendance of roughly 90% is usually required to keep it).
  • A free toolkit on completion for many trades.
  • A recognised certificate that helps with local and overseas jobs.
  • Hands-on labs, soft-skills training and, in some centres, industry exposure.

Who is eligible?

  • You are a Pakistani citizen with a valid CNIC (or Form-B if under 18 for certain trades).
  • Age is generally between 18 and 45 years (some IT/advanced courses set their own age band).
  • Minimum Matriculation for most trades; Intermediate or a degree for IT and advanced trades.
  • Open to both men and women from all provinces, AJK and GB.

Popular trades offered

  • IT & digital: web development, graphic/UI design, digital marketing, freelancing, data science, AI, cybersecurity, cloud computing.
  • Technical & vocational: electrical, HVAC/air-conditioning, welding, solar & renewable energy, plumbing, auto.
  • Other: construction trades, healthcare assistance, beauty & wellness, fashion designing.

Documents you'll need

  • CNIC or Form-B.
  • Educational certificates / marks sheets.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • A working email address and mobile number for the portal.

How to apply — step by step

  1. Open the official NSIS portal

    When a new batch is open, go to the official NAVTTC registration portal at nsis.navttc.gov.pk. Avoid third-party "agents".

  2. Register an account

    Create your account using your CNIC, email address and mobile number.

  3. Choose your trade and institute

    Pick a trade, a training institute near you, your city and the course duration.

  4. Upload your documents

    Attach your CNIC/Form-B, educational certificates and a passport-size photo. Make sure scans are clear.

  5. Submit and save your application number

    Review everything, submit, and save your application number so you can track your status on the dashboard.

  6. Watch for the merit list & report to the centre

    If shortlisted you'll usually be notified by SMS. Take your original documents and join on the assigned date.

Frequently asked questions

Are NAVTTC courses really free?

Yes. NAVTTC courses under the Prime Minister's Youth Skill Development Program are 100% free and funded by the Government of Pakistan. Many trades also pay a monthly stipend and give a toolkit and a recognised certificate on completion. You should never pay an agent to apply.

Who can apply for NAVTTC free courses?

Any Pakistani citizen with a valid CNIC or Form-B can apply. Most trades require a minimum of Matriculation and an age of roughly 18 to 45 years, while IT and advanced trades may require Intermediate or a degree. Both men and women from all provinces are eligible.

What courses does NAVTTC offer?

NAVTTC offers IT and digital skills such as web development, digital marketing, freelancing, data science, AI and cybersecurity, plus technical and vocational trades like electrical, HVAC, welding, solar/renewable energy, construction, healthcare and fashion design.

How do I apply online?

Register on the official NSIS portal at nsis.navttc.gov.pk using your CNIC, email and mobile number. Choose your trade and institute, upload your CNIC and educational certificates, submit, and save your application number. Selected candidates are notified, usually by SMS.
