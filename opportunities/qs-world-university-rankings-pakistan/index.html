Pakistan in the QS Asia Rankings 2026

QS also publishes a regional ranking for Asia. In the QS Asia University Rankings 2026, NUST Islamabad placed highest among Pakistani institutions at around 68th in Asia, followed by Quaid-i-Azam University near 89th, LUMS around 129th, COMSATS near 150th, and the University of the Punjab around 151st.

What the QS ranking measures

QS scores universities across several indicators rather than a single number. These include academic reputation (the largest factor), employer reputation, faculty-to-student ratio, citations per faculty, and the international faculty and student ratios, alongside newer measures such as sustainability, employment outcomes and international research collaboration. Because academic and employer reputation carry significant weight, large research universities with strong global networks — like NUST and QAU — tend to rank highest in Pakistan.

How to use rankings wisely

A ranking is a useful signal, not a verdict. Before you choose where to study or where to apply for a scholarship, look beyond the overall number:

  • Compare by subject, not just overall. A university ranked lower overall can be the strongest choice for your specific field — for example, FAST-NUCES and NUST SEECS are widely respected for computer science, and AKU for medicine.
  • Check fees and scholarships. A higher-ranked private university may cost much more; many top public universities offer need-based support such as the HEC Need-Based Scholarship.
  • Weigh location, faculty and outcomes. Graduate employment, faculty quality, and access to internships often matter more for your career than a few ranking places.
  • Use more than one ranking. QS, Times Higher Education and others measure different things and can rank the same university differently.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the top-ranked university in Pakistan in QS 2026?

In the QS World University Rankings 2026, Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU) in Islamabad is Pakistan's highest-ranked university, placed around 354 globally, followed closely by NUST at around 371. Both are based in Islamabad.

How many Pakistani universities are in the QS World Rankings 2026?

Eighteen Pakistani universities were included in the QS World University Rankings 2026. In the separate QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026, as many as 35 Pakistani universities were featured across different subjects.

What does the QS World University Ranking measure?

QS ranks universities using indicators such as academic reputation, employer reputation, faculty-to-student ratio, citations per faculty, and international faculty and student ratios, along with newer measures like sustainability and employment outcomes. Academic reputation carries the largest weight.

Should I choose a university only by its QS ranking?

No. Rankings are one useful signal, but you should also weigh your subject's strength, fees, location, faculty, scholarships and graduate outcomes. A university ranked lower overall may be excellent for your particular field, so compare by programme, not just overall position.
